Why this meeting matters

Availability beats another hard case.

  • People forget dry bags. A rigid case only works if it came on the trip.
  • D14 lives in the kit: packs to ~40% of any rigid Nereid shell — disappears until water shows up.
  • Survey-aligned: waterproof + phone usable through the window — not a dead pouch.
  • Without a soft-goods manufacturing path, we only know how to machine boxes. The packable SKU — and the campus dunk demo story — dies on paper.

The engineering bet

Hard where it senses. Soft everywhere else.

500D aramid + TPU · RF-welded to bezel lip · 3 rolls + buckle · A1 load-cell glass

Rigid-only path

  • Always the same bulk in the bag
  • Impact goes into the shell
  • Seal = O-rings on mating halves
  • Great for abuse; poor for “bring it every time”

D14 path

  • Fabric body absorbs hit by deforming
  • Only hard part = force-sensed window bezel
  • Closure = trained roll + dual side-release clamp
  • Manufacturing owns RF weld + fold discipline + bezel FAIR

Integrity

We will not oversell IPX claims.

After P1 pass we claim

  • Bench dunk survival at 1.5× design
  • Documented RF-weld + fold process
  • Repeatable peel / fold / dunk logs
  • “Soft-goods architecture validated for demos”

We will not claim yet

  • IPX8 / dive-computer certification
  • Life-critical or overhead diving use
  • Unlimited depth or “military waterproof”
  • Production yield or lifetime abrasion rating

Campus dunk demos only happen on serials that passed P1 proof. No exceptions for “it looked fine.”
