Why D14

  • Only catalog design that packs flat — soft body + rigid force-sensed window.
  • Solves availability: comes in the bag; three rolls + buckle seal.
  • Survey-aligned: waterproof + touch through glass (4 load cells).

Evidence already in hand

  • CAD: models/D14/ bezel · window · fabric pattern
  • Full BOM, mass stack, 9-phase process, work instructions
  • HQ visuals + product film + deployment sequence

P1 success / kill

  • Success: ≥5 units dunk-pass 1.5×; peel + fold logs signed.
  • Kill: flange leak or fold cannot seal → stop before production tooling.

Not approved by this vote

  • Production bezel injection mold
  • 100–250 unit inventory buy
  • IPX8 / “military waterproof” claims
