Why D15

  • Only catalog design engineered past recreational depth via oil pressure compensation (ΔP ≈ 0), not thicker walls.
  • 1 mm PPA shell + rolling diaphragm + dielectric silicone oil + ≥40 mL thermal reservoir.
  • Honest trade: phone screen unusable in oil; UI = sealed OLED + physical keys.

Evidence already in hand

  • CAD STEP/STL: models/D15/
  • Full BOM, mass stack, 9-phase process, work instructions
  • HQ visuals + product film for training

P1 success / kill

  • Success: ≥3 units hold 15 bar / 15 min; oil mass logged ±5 g; process sheet signed.
  • Kill: cannot achieve proof without redesign → stop before mold steel.

Explicitly not approved by this vote

  • Production injection molds
  • 100-unit inventory buy
  • Consumer “100 m certified” claims
