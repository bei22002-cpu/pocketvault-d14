The product

  • Soft TPU-aramid body, rigid glass bezel, 3-fold roll-and-clamp.
  • Use the phone through the window. Packs flat with lanyard.
  • Drawing D14-RT-001 · packed 220×150×25 mm · ~210 g.

Customer evidence (n = 47)

  • Hangtag: waterproof · touchscreen · easy open · durable.
  • Price band $15–$25; indifference ≈ $20 → MSRP $19.99.
  • Soft unaided intent → in-store dunk demo is required.

Who we sell to

  • Primary customer = the store, not Instagram.
  • Competitor is a Ziploc and “I’ll be careful” — not an $80 dry bag.
  • First doors: campus bookstore, outdoor, water shop, convenience.
  • Opening order: case of 12. Reorder by text at 3 left.

Known weaknesses (stated up front)

  • Unaided intent at $19.99 is soft: 34% top-2 → demo-led selling, not packaging.
  • Sample is 81% students → campus-adjacent doors first.
  • Economics are modeled ±30–50%, not quoted. The RFQ is part of the ask.
