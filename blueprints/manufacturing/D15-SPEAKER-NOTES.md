## Timed script

### Slide 1 — Title (45 sec)
“I’m asking for one decision: authorize **P1 manufacturing** for D15 Oilfill-100 — CNC housings, oil-fill process, and hydrostatic proof — so we can prove depth physics before we spend mold money.”

### Slide 2 — Stakes (60 sec)
“Recreational cases stop at snorkel-to-sport depths by thickening walls. D15 is the jump to **100 m**. Without a manufacturing path it’s a render. With P1, we find out if the architecture is real.”

### Slide 3 — Insight (90 sec)
“The bet is equalize, don’t fight. Rolling diaphragm compresses silicone oil until shell ΔP is ~0. That lets us keep **1 mm walls**. Honest trade: phone screen dies in oil; UI is sealed OLED + keys.”

*Pause. Let them object here if they hate the trade — better now than later.*

### Slide 4 — Spec (45 sec)
“Spec is frozen enough to machine: envelope, port, OLED bay, mass budget 178 g. CAD STEP is already exported.”

### Slide 5 — Evidence (45 sec)
“We’re not pitching a sketch. Geometry, BOM, mass stack, explode stills, and a product film exist. This meeting is about **authorization to prove**, not invention.”

### Slide 6 — Math (75 sec)
“Two numbers matter: **≥40 mL reservoir** from thermal expansion math, and **15 bar proof** every unit. Undersize the reservoir and heat kills you. Skip proof and we are lying about depth.”

### Slide 7 — Ladder (75 sec) ★
“Critical: **I am not asking for mold steel today.** P1 is the cheap fail. P3 tooling is a *separate* vote after proof.”

### Slide 8 — Phases (40 sec)
“Nine phases, each with an exit gate. Air leak **before** oil. That’s how we don’t waste oil diagnosing a bad O-ring.”

### Slide 9 — Money (60 sec)
“Authorization band **$10–28k**, planning midpoint **~$18k**. Explicitly excluded: production molds, inventory, certification filing.”

### Slide 10 — Schedule (40 sec)
“Six weeks to a go/no-go memo. Kill criterion: cannot hold 15 bar. Success: ≥3 units pass with logged oil mass.”

### Slide 11 — Risks (60 sec)
“Walk the table. Oil/phone chemistry, diaphragm fatigue, UX rejection of no touchscreen — each has a P1 mitigation. We named the killers before you did.”

### Slide 12 — Integrity (45 sec)
“After P1 we claim bench-validated architecture — **not** a consumer 100 m cert. Overclaiming is how programs get shut down later. We won’t.”

### Slide 13 — Ask (60 sec)
Read the Approve box verbatim. Then: “If you hold because you reject depth as a thesis, say that. Don’t hold because the plan isn’t ready — it is.”

### Slide 14 — Close (20 sec)
“Make pressure disappear — then prove it. Yes or no on P1?”

**Stop talking. Let silence work.**

## Likely Q&A (have answers cold)

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| “Why not just thicken the case?” | Mass and optic cost explode; still a dry vessel. Compensation is the only light path to 100 m. |
| “Phone in oil will brick.” | P1 includes sacrificial soak + CoC oil only. If chemistry fails, we kill before tooling. |
| “Nobody wants a device without a phone screen.” | Possible — that’s why OLED UX is a P2 gate before mold steel. Depth buyers accept instruments; we test that. |
| “$28k is a lot for students / early stage.” | Compared to a bad mold ($8–25k) that leaks, P1 is insurance. Ceiling, not target. |
| “Can FDM prove this?” | No. FDM is P0 fit only. Saying otherwise is how you fail the pot and lose trust. |
| “Is this certified?” | No. We will not claim certification. P1 proves physics and process. |
| “Why D15 not Rolltop?” | Different jobs. Rolltop is always-with-you dry carry. D15 is depth. This vote is the depth manufacturing path. |
| “What if P1 fails?” | We stop. Write the autopsy. No P3. That *is* success of the governance model. |
