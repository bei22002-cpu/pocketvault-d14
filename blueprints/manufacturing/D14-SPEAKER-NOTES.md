## Timed script

### Slide 1 — Title (45 sec)
“I’m asking for one decision: authorize **P1 manufacturing** for D14 Rolltop — bezels, RF-weld fabric bodies, window stack, and dunk proof — so we prove the soft-goods seal before production tooling.”

### Slide 2 — Stakes (60 sec)
“People forget dry bags. Rigid cases only work if they came on the trip. D14 packs to ~40% of a hard shell and still lets you use the phone through glass. Without a soft-goods path, we only know how to machine boxes.”

### Slide 3 — Insight (90 sec)
“Hard where it senses, soft everywhere else. TPU-aramid RF-welded to a force-sensed bezel. Three rolls and dual buckles. Manufacturing owns the weld and the fold — not wall thickness.”

### Slide 4 — Spec (45 sec)
“Spec is frozen enough to cut fabric and machine flanges: 175×95×40, 148×70 aperture, 122 g mass stack.”

### Slide 5 — Evidence (45 sec)
“CAD, BOM, mass, explode stills, lifestyle pack shot, film — already exist. This vote is authorization to prove, not invention.”

### Slide 6 — Process (75 sec)
“Two product truths: **RF weld integrity** and **fold discipline**. Undersell either and the dunk demo becomes a public failure.”

### Slide 7 — Ladder (75 sec) ★
“I am **not** asking for production dies today. P1 is the cheap fail. P3 tooling is a separate vote after dunk evidence.”

### Slide 8 — Phases (40 sec)
“Nine phases, exit-gated. Fold training before dunk. Peel coupons every weld lot.”

### Slide 9 — Money (60 sec)
“Authorization **$7–18k**, midpoint **~$11k**. Excluded: production molds, inventory, certification filing.”

### Slide 10 — Schedule (40 sec)
“Five weeks to go/no-go. Kill: flange leak or fold can’t seal. Success: ≥5 dunk-pass with logs.”

### Slide 11 — Risks (60 sec)
“Walk the table — pinholes, under-rolling, bezel edge, wet touch, abrasion, COGS vs $19.99. Each has a P1 mitigation.”

### Slide 12 — Integrity (45 sec)
“After P1 we claim demo-ready architecture — **not** IPX8. Dunk demos only on passed serials.”

### Slide 13 — Ask (60 sec)
Read Approve box. Then: “Hold only if you reject soft-goods as a thesis — not because the plan isn’t ready.”

### Slide 14 — Close (20 sec)
“Pack flat. Roll shut. Prove the weld. Yes or no on P1?”

**Stop talking.**

## Likely Q&A

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| “Just buy generic dry bags.” | They fog, don’t sense touch, and aren’t a product story. D14 is usable through glass + packable. |
| “RF weld is exotic / expensive.” | It’s standard soft-goods waterproofing. P1 funds shop time to prove our flange geometry. |
| “Users won’t roll three times.” | That’s why fold marks + training card are P1 deliverables — and a kill risk we named. |
| “Bezel will crack.” | Known weak point. P1 includes bumper options and drop samples before we call structure done. |
| “Why not D15 depth instead?” | Different thesis. D14 is availability + campus dunk demos. D15 is depth moat. Separate votes. |
| “Can we sew instead of RF?” | Stitch holes below waterline are a leak factory. RF or heat-weld only for P1. |
| “$18k for fabric?” | Ceiling includes bezels, cells, glass, electronics, dunk — not just cloth. Midpoint ~$11k. |
| “Is this IPX8?” | No. We will not claim that from P1. |

## Closing line if the room stalls

“I’m not asking you to believe the bag never leaks. I’m asking you to fund the dunks that can prove the weld — before we ever demo on campus.”
