The problem

Phones die in water. Dive computers don’t show your apps.

Nereid puts a sealed dry-air pocket over the phone’s own screen, with a water-immune sensing layer on the outside. One physics stack — many interfaces.

  • Capacitive touch fails underwater and through gloves
  • Hard cases usually kill the screen as an input surface
  • Mission profiles need different trade-offs: weight, depth, precision, hands-free
Shared architecture

Same envelope. Different signal path.

Every card in the catalog fixes on sensing architecture, construction, a weight budget from material densities, sealing, and the one trade-off that decides whether you’d choose it.
