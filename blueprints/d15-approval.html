Freeze-before-tooling math

The reservoir is non-negotiable.

If we under-size expansion volume, the case bulges or spits seals in heat. This is the number that kills programs that “just fill it with oil.”

Thermal expansion

V_oil ≈ 400 mL

CTE ≈ 0.00095 / °C (silicone oil)

ΔT = 71 − (−32) = 103 °C

ΔV ≈ 400 × 0.00095 × 103 ≈ 39 mL

Spec floor: ≥40 mL free + 25% margin → ~50 mL design

Pressure proof

Design: 100 m → 10 bar

Proof (every unit): 15 bar × 15 min

Working hold: 10 bar × 30 min + UI exercise

Pass = no weep, no diaphragm tear, no OLED fog, keys still actuate.

FDM shells are explicitly excluded from this gate.
