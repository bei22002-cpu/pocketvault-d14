What changed in the world

Phones went everywhere water is, and protection never followed.

  • 55% of surveyed buyers have already ruined something in water.
  • 62% carry no protection at all — the default is hope.
  • Dry bags solved the wrong half: they protect the phone by making it unusable.
  • People will not carry gear that costs them the camera.
PocketVault survey · n = 47 · campus-weighted · Sep 2026

The product

D14 seals like a dry bag and works like a phone.

  • Waterproof — the #1 ranked claim in our survey.
  • Touchscreen through the window — the reason it beats a bag.
  • Packs flat with a wrist lanyard — a counter SKU, not a shelf hog.
  • Obvious close — roll three times, clip two buckles.

What this decision actually costs

Three trade-offs the room has to weigh — none of them are free.

1 · Flagship vs. price point

D14 as drawn carries glass and four load cells and models ~$60–$90 landed. That cannot be a $19.99 case pack. We ship a de-featured store SKU and keep the instrumented unit as the demo.

2 · Working capital

Cases bought are cash frozen until they sell through. We pre-sell opening orders before committing a large factory PO — slower start, far smaller hole if we are wrong.

3 · One SKU, not three

Adopting D14 shelves the wallet roll-up and the rigid case family for this window. That is the real cost of focus, and it should be said out loud.

Claim discipline · no IPX8 until certified

Why

  • D14-RT-001 drawing freeze

  • $19.99 impulse SKU at about keystone
  • ~50% margin at ~$10 wholesale
  • Compact counter / PDQ footprint
  • Dunk demo drives sell-through
  • Simple reorder, low risk opening order
For shoppers — the end user
  • Protect phone, wallet, keys near water
  • Use the phone through the window
  • Beats Ziploc and nothing — 62% use nothing
Shelf claims — survey-ranked
  • Waterproof · touchscreen · easy open · durable

Customer Relationships

  • Opening order → biweekly reorder check-in
  • COD first PO; Net-15 / 30 once trusted
  • First-case swap offer

Channels

  • Reorder by text
