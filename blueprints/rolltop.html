We're shipping the roll-up bag

From the Nereid catalog and the wallet sketch, IBC locks on one SKU: a slim card wallet with a permanently attached roll-top dry bag.

Why this
It's already on you — solves “I forget my dry bag,” the survey’s real availability problem.
Price band
$19.99 list · $14.99 early-bird (survey sweet spot).
P0 features
IPX7 dunk seal · packs to ~15 mm · holds 6.9″ phone + keys + cash.
Not in v1
Clear touchscreen window (survey want) → v1.1 if demos demand it.

Four moves

Stowed in a pocket. Deployed in seconds. No separate pouch to remember.
